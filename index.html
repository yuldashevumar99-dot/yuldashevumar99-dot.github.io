Screen Scanner

Screen-to-text for Windows · Free, $0.99 Pro

Press Alt+C, drag over any part of the screen, and the text is on your clipboard. It runs the OCR built into Windows, fully offline.

Hard part Electron can’t reach the Windows OCR or Store purchase APIs on its own. I wrote a small C# helper that does both, and the app calls it for every scan and for the Pro unlock.

  • Electron
  • React
  • C# / WinRT
  • Store in-app purchase
